Enhanced Generalized Logarithmic Loss for Pansharpening: A Curvature-Distribution Matching Perspective
Pansharpening aims to fuse high-resolution panchromatic (PAN) images with low-resolution multispectral (LRMS) images to produce high-quality high-resolution multispectral (HRMS) images, also known as fusion images. Recent advances in deep learning have demonstrated superior performance in this field. However, classic loss functions, such as Mean Squared Error (MSE) and Mean Absolute Error (MAE), struggle to balance spatial detail preservation and spectral fidelity. To address this limitation, we propose a Generalized Logarithmic Loss (GLL), which can better adapt to complex error distributions encountered in pansharpening. Specifically, GLL increases penalties for small errors to improve detail preservation, while employing a globally smooth loss curve to reduce sensitivity to large errors, thereby enhancing the quality of the fused images. Curvature analysis within the Bregman divergence framework, coupled with a curvature-distribution matching index, quantifies GLL's adaptability to heavy-tailed error distributions. Building on this, an Enhanced Generalized Logarithmic Loss (EGLL) is introduced, integrating GLL with gradient-consistent and L1 loss terms to enhance texture preservation and global consistency, making it well-suited for pansharpening tasks. Extensive experiments demonstrate that EGLL significantly outperforms other loss functions, exhibiting strong generalization across various network architectures and datasets.
