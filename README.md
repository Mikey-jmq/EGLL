Enhanced Generalized Logarithmic Loss for Pansharpening: A Curvature-Distribution Matching Perspective
Pansharpening aims to fuse high-resolution panchromatic (PAN) images with low-resolution multispectral (LRMS) images to generate high-quality high-resolution multispectral (HRMS) images, namely fused images. Recent advances in deep learning have achieved superior performance in this field. However, conventional loss functions, such as Mean Squared Error (MSE) and Mean Absolute Error (MAE), are difficult to balance spatial detail preservation and spectral fidelity. To alleviate this limitation, we propose a Generalized Logarithmic Loss (GLL), which exhibits better adaptability to the complex error distributions in pansharpening. Specifically, GLL imposes higher penalties on small errors to improve detail preservation, while adopting a globally smooth loss curve to reduce sensitivity to large errors, thus improving the quality of fused images. Theoretically, based on Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE), we devise a Hessian-distribution matching index. By associating the geometric curvature of the loss function with Fisher information, this index rigorously ensures the superior adaptability of GLL to long-tailed error distributions through a tighter estimation variance bound. Furthermore, we develop an Enhanced Generalized Logarithmic Loss (EGLL), which integrates GLL with gradient-consistent and L1 loss terms to strengthen texture preservation and global consistency, rendering it highly suitable for pansharpening tasks. Extensive experiments validate that EGLL significantly outperforms conventional loss functions on multiple representative pansharpening benchmark datasets.
